HOW TO PLAY

A / D / ← →
Lane Switching
Shift between Left, Center, and Right lanes (or swipe left/right on touch).
SPACE / ↑ / W
Variable Jump & Double Jump
Hold longer for higher jumps. Press again while airborne to double jump over tall hazards.
S / ↓
Slide Under Barriers
Lowers hitbox to slip under high lasers and overhead platforms. Also acts as a fast aerial drop.
SHIFT / G
Gravity Inversion Flip
Flip between the floor and ceiling track! Essential when the entire floor is impassable.
CTRL / F
Nitro Super Boost
Charge energy by collecting gold coins. Grants blazing speed and invulnerability to obstacles!
BOSS FIGHT
Mini-Boss Encounters
Every 400m, a floating sentinel attacks! Auto-blasters engage while you dodge its plasma spheres.
