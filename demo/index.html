HOW TO PLAY

SPACE / ↑
Variable Jump & Double Jump
Hold key/tap longer for higher jumps. Press again in air to double jump over large obstacles.
S / ↓
Slide Under Obstacles
Lowers hitbox to slip under high lasers, hovering barriers, and rotating crushers.
SHIFT / G
Gravity Inversion Flip
Instantly flip from floor to ceiling! Essential when the entire ground lane is blocked.
CTRL / F
Nitro Super Boost
Charge energy by collecting blue orbs & coins. Boosts speed & renders you invulnerable!
500m BOSS
Mini-Boss Encounters
Dodge boss attacks while your runner auto-fires plasma bolts. Huge loot drop upon victory!
